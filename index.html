108TEA Club

We host quiet, friendly tea ceremonies where good teas are shared and conversations unfold naturally. The teas come from the catalog above — thoughtfully selected and meant to be enjoyed slowly. Whether you are new to tea or already on the path, you are welcome.

How to join

Send a short hello on Telegram and tell us you’d like to join a ceremony.

Gatherings are small and unhurried. We’ll share time and place privately.

Nothing to bring — just curiosity and time. If it’s your first visit, we’ll guide you through the flow and teas we drink.

Stay tuned, take care, and enjoy your tea sessions.
