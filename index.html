Learn / Brewing Guide

There’s no single “correct” way to brew tea. Start simple and let the leaf guide you.

  1. Cup method: put a pinch of tea in a cup, add hot water, sip slowly.
  2. Gaiwan (gong fu): 3–7 g per 100–120 ml; short infusions, taste and adjust.
  3. Fire / kettle: boil water on flame, keep a gentle simmer, brew in a pot for a deeper, warming cup.

We cover details at ceremonies and will add links with guides soon.

General tips

  • Water: 80–100 °C depending on the tea
  • Rinse (Pu’er): 5–10 sec
  • Infusions: 10–30 sec; shorter for lighter teas
  • Breathe, pour, listen — repeat
