Ceremonies

We host regular tea & music sessions in Berlin — small circles focused on mindful brewing and listening. Attendance is limited; announcements are posted in Telegram.

  • Location: Zugarten / U / S‑Bahn Zoo area
  • Format: 2–3 teas, music, quiet conversation
  • Booking via Telegram DM
Upcoming Sessions

Good to know

  • Arrive 5–10 minutes early
  • Phones on silent
  • Light snacks after the session

Learn / Brewing Guide

  1. Water 80–100 °C depending on tea type
  2. Leaf: 3–7 g per 100–120 ml
  3. Rinse (Pu’er): 5–10 sec
  4. Infusions: 10–30 sec, adjust to taste
  5. Breathe, pour, listen — repeat
