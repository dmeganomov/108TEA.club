108TEA Club

About

A quiet place in Berlin for unhurried tea.

We brew Chinese teas gongfu-style, gather in small circles, and let conversations unfold at their own pace.

Ritual & Atmosphere

Each ceremony is an invitation to return to the present moment. Through the shifting aroma of warm leaves, the taste that evolves with every steep, and the quiet rhythm of brewing, attention naturally softens and refocuses.

The ritual is not about formality — it’s about observing, listening, and letting awareness settle. In this gentle state, tea becomes a way to experience time differently: slower, deeper, more alive.

To support this space, we create subtle drone–ambient–ethno–psy soundscapes — live jam sessions that flow with the ceremony, blending seamlessly with silence and breath. The sound doesn’t lead; it drifts, holds, and expands the moment.

Booking

We host intimate and friendly tea ceremonies where good teas are shared and conversations unfold naturally.

All teas are thoughtfully selected from our collection and meant to be enjoyed slowly. Whether you are new to tea or already on the path, you are welcome to join.

Events & Collaboration

We are open to collaborations — musical or artistic evenings, tea performances, creative gatherings with musicians and artists, as well as off-site ceremonies and pop-up events.
